About MilletMetaDB

MilletMetaDB is a comprehensive database dedicated to major millet-derived metabolites; it contains experimentally validated compounds associated with stress responses. The database highlights metabolite expression profiles under biotic and abiotic stress conditions. Each entry includes biological activity, chemical structure, and target metabolite information. MilletMetaDB serves as a valuable resource for stress biology research and crop improvement in millets.

About Tamil Nadu Agricultural University (TNAU)

Established in 1971, TNAU is one of India's foremost agricultural universities. It has played a significant role in enhancing food security and agricultural productivity in the country, known for its excellence in education, research, and extension. TNAU offers a diverse array of academic programs and houses multiple research centers, including the renowned CPMB&B. The university stands as a pillar of agricultural advancement in India.

About the Centre for Plant Molecular Biology & Biotechnology (CPMB&B)

CPMB&B is a premier center within TNAU dedicated to cutting-edge research in plant molecular biology, genomics, and biotechnology. The centre focuses on solving major 26/166 challenges in agriculture through biotechnological interventions and advanced genomic tools. With highly experienced faculty and state-of-the-art facilities, CPMB&B is a hub of innovation and academic excellence in plant science.
